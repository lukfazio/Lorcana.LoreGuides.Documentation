# Terms and Translation Guardrails

This document defines the terms that must remain in English for AI agents, even when generating or localizing content into other languages. These guardrails ensure that official product names, game terms, and brand-specific terminology are preserved in their original English form.

## AI Guardrail Rules

- Preserve each listed term in English exactly as written.
- Do not create or apply unofficial translations for these terms.
- Keep original casing and spelling.
- If a term appears inside a longer sentence, leave the term itself in English.
- When a term must remain in English, optionally include a translation of that term in parentheses immediately after it to help the reader understand the meaning.
- Do not replace these terms with analogous words in the target language.
- When in doubt, retain the English original and verify against official localization references.

### Other Target Languages
- Follow the same rule: use only officially documented localizations.
- If no official localized term exists for a specific language, keep the English term.
- Do not invent localized forms for brand names, game names, or specialized terminology.
 

## Notes

These guardrails are designed for AI agents working with translations, localization, or multilingual content generation. The objective is to protect the integrity of official terms by keeping the original English form unless a validated, language-specific exception exists.
